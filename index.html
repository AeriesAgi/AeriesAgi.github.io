05 / OPERATING PRINCIPLES

The rules underneath the work.

01

Human authority remains explicit.

AI can surface options, explain risk and prepare actions. Accountable people approve consequential decisions.

02

Resilience is a product feature.

Fallbacks, degraded connectivity and incomplete inputs are designed for instead of being treated as edge cases.

03

Status labels stay honest.

Prototype, pilot-ready, connector-ready and production are different states. The distinction is kept visible.

04

Evidence closes the loop.

Tests, approvals, audit trails and release records matter more than a polished claim with nothing behind it.
